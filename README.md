# NEOKODL-Notenverwaltung
## "Notenverwaltung von Enderlon ohne Kreuzer oba dafir mitn Loris"

The NEOKODL-Notenverwaltung is cool new tool for teachers to excel at school B-).
Take some notes, write something down, this app is gonna fundamentally change how you see your students. 
Through the patented Student-Judging-Technology™ this app is able to enable teachers to unlock their full potential while archiving every single one of their students blunders! _...and also the positive things_.
And all that at the tips of your fingers!
